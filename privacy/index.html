MTG Scan Build Privacy Policy

Developer: MTG Scan Build
Last updated: October 8, 2026
Contact: sbatyapps@gmail.com

Pre-release policy draft. Production advertisements and paid Pro checkout are not enabled. Account sign-in, purchase verification, restoration, and account deletion are still being implemented. The sections describing those planned services are not claims that they are available. This policy must be finalized against the completed release before public launch.

1. What the app does

MTG Scan Build is an unofficial Android tool for scanning Magic: The Gathering cards, managing collections and decks, and viewing news, legality information, and rule references. It is not affiliated with Wizards of the Coast.

2. Your collection and device settings

Collection entries, quantities, folder names and colors, saved decks, and app settings are stored on your device. The app does not currently upload your collection to a developer-operated cloud database. Android backup may include app data depending on your device settings and backup provider.

CSV import and export operate on files you choose. Exports and deck lists are shared only when you choose a destination or another app. Once shared, those files are handled by that destination's privacy practices.

3. Camera, photos, and sound files

Camera access is used for card recognition. The app uses Google ML Kit for on-device text recognition and does not upload camera frames or selected photos to the developer. Recognized card names and, when available, set codes and collector numbers are sent to Scryfall to retrieve card information. ML Kit and Google services may download components and process technical SDK information under Google's policies.

The app accesses photos or custom scan sounds that you select through Android's file picker. Camera permission can be revoked in Android settings. No microphone, contacts, or precise-location permission is requested by the app.

4. Online card data, news, and external links

Requests to Scryfall can include card names, printing identifiers, format filters, and search terms. Moxfield requests can include searches, format choices, and deck IDs or links you enter. News requests go to Wizards of the Coast. Network providers receive your IP address and ordinary request metadata. Tapping articles, rules, store links, or other external links opens services governed by their own policies.

Card pictures and card-name information may be cached locally. You can clear the image cache from Settings. The app does not sell your collection data.

5. Advertising and privacy choices

Basic is intended to show small Google AdMob banners on non-Scan pages. The private developer build excludes the advertising SDK. Paid Pro is intended to remove banners once its verified purchase system is available.

During development, the public debug build uses Google test advertisements. Production advertising is currently disabled. Google Mobile Ads and the User Messaging Platform are included in the public build. When enabled and permitted, these services may process IP addresses, device and advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from an IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic information. Non-personalized advertisements do not mean no data is processed.

The app stores only an age category locally, not a birth date. No advertisements are requested for an unknown category or an under-13 selection. Ages 13-17 receive under-age-of-consent treatment; all ad requests are configured as non-personalized with a PG maximum content rating. The intended audience is ages 13 and older.

Ad requests wait for a successful Google privacy-information update and the SDK's permission to request ads. Where required, Google presents a privacy form. A Manage ad privacy choices option appears in Settings when the SDK requires it. Age category and connection retry controls are also in Settings.

6. Firebase accounts and paid Pro - planned

The public build is configured for Firebase Authentication, Firestore, and Cloud Functions. Google sign-in is configured in Firebase, but there is no account sign-in flow in the app yet. The initial Firestore rules deny client access. Google Analytics is not included in this integration.

The planned account system will use a Firebase user ID and Google account information, such as an email address and display name, to identify the account. The planned backend will verify Google Play purchase tokens and store account-linked purchase and Pro entitlement records for restoration and fraud prevention. We do not receive your full payment-card details; Google Play processes payment.

Before enabling accounts and purchases, we will finalize account-deletion controls, the external deletion-request page, retention periods, purchase-record treatment, and the Play Data safety declarations. These are outstanding release requirements.

7. Deletion and retention in the current build

You can remove collection entries, folders, and decks through the app. Removing a folder leaves its cards in the collection. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local app data; exported files and Android/cloud backups may remain separately. Manage those copies through their file or backup provider.

Technical data handled by Google and other providers is subject to their retention practices. This app does not yet operate a live account/purchase-record backend. For privacy questions, contact sbatyapps@gmail.com. Do not send passwords, payment details, or service-account keys.
