Delete your MTG Scan Build account

Developer: MTG Scan Build
Contact: sbatyapps@gmail.com

Pre-release instructions. The account-deletion backend has been deployed for release testing, but the updated account-enabled app has not been released through Google Play. The workflow below must be tested before launch. You may contact support now with questions or a request; this page does not claim that the release workflow has been validated.

Request deletion without installing the app

Email sbatyapps@gmail.com with the subject MTG Scan Build account deletion. State that you want your MTG Scan Build account deleted and provide the email address of the Google account you used to sign in. You do not need to reinstall the app.

We will verify account ownership before deleting an account. Providing an email address alone is not proof of ownership: support will require a confirmation through the registered address and match the request to the app account. We will contact you if further verification is needed and confirm completion once deletion finishes.

Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, identity documents, payment-card details, or purchase tokens. A deletion request cannot transfer a purchase to someone else's Google account.

In the account-enabled app

Open the Pro comparison page, select Delete app account, read the confirmation, and confirm your identity using the same Google account. An internet connection is required. Cancellation of identity confirmation does not request deletion. If a network error interrupts the request, server cleanup may still complete; check the status before retrying or contact support.

What is deleted and what stays

  • Your Firebase app account and its account profile are deleted, including its stored email address and display name. Account-linked Pro verification records are removed or detached as described below.
  • In-app deletion signs you out and clears the device's cached Pro access. A remote support request cannot erase storage on your device; sign out or clear app storage locally to remove local sign-in/cache data.
  • Your local cards, folders, saved decks, and settings are kept. The developer does not receive or delete this on-device collection. Use the app's deletion controls or Android's clear-storage control to remove it. Exported files and backups must be removed from their own storage providers.
  • For purchase recovery and fraud prevention, we retain a hashed Google-account identifier, purchase token, and verification status while the permanent Pro purchase remains valid. These records do not include your email, display name, or collection. Canceled or revoked retained purchases are removed after server verification; service failures may delay that check.
  • A temporary deletion marker contains the Firebase account ID while cleanup is pending. After completion, it contains only a hash of that ID and the completion time. The hourly cleanup removes completed markers after 24 hours; service interruptions may delay removal.
  • Support correspondence remains in the support mailbox until the request is resolved, then is deleted from the developer's mailbox. Email-provider backups, service logs, and Google Play transaction records are governed by the respective provider's retention practices.

Deleting the app account does not delete your Google account or automatically refund a Google Play purchase. A valid permanent Pro purchase can be recovered by signing in again with the same Google account and restoring the purchase, after Google Play verifies it.
