MTG Scan Build Privacy Policy

Developer: MTG Scan Build
Last updated: October 8, 2026

Pre-release policy draft. Production advertisements and production Pro checkout are not enabled. A separate internal checkout build is being prepared for Play license-tester validation. Account sign-in and deletion have been tested on the internal Play release; paid verification and restoration still require purchase testing. These internal tests are not a public launch. This policy must be finalized against the completed release before public launch.

1. What the app does

MTG Scan Build is an unofficial Android tool for scanning Magic: The Gathering cards, managing collections and decks, and viewing news, legality information, and rule references. It is not affiliated with Wizards of the Coast.

2. Your collection and device settings

Collection entries, quantities, folder names and colors, saved decks, and app settings are stored on your device. The app does not currently upload your collection to a developer-operated cloud database. Android backup may include app data depending on your device settings and backup provider.

CSV import and export operate on files you choose. Exports and deck lists are shared only when you choose a destination or another app. Once shared, those files are handled by that destination's privacy practices.

3. Camera, photos, and sound files

Camera access is used for card recognition. The app uses Google ML Kit for on-device text recognition and does not upload camera frames or selected photos to the developer. Recognized card names and, when available, set codes and collector numbers are sent to Scryfall to retrieve card information. ML Kit and Google services may download components and process technical SDK information under Google's policies.

The app accesses photos or custom scan sounds that you select through Android's file picker. Camera permission can be revoked in Android settings. No microphone, contacts, or precise-location permission is requested by the app.

4. Online card data, news, and external links

Requests to Scryfall can include card names, printing identifiers, format filters, and search terms. Moxfield requests can include searches, format choices, and deck IDs or links you enter. News requests go to Wizards of the Coast. Network providers receive your IP address and ordinary request metadata. Tapping articles, rules, store links, or other external links opens services governed by their own policies.

Card pictures and card-name information may be cached locally. You can clear the image cache from Settings. The app does not sell your collection data.

5. Advertising and privacy choices

Basic is intended to show small Google AdMob banners on non-Scan pages. The private developer build excludes the advertising SDK. Paid Pro is intended to remove banners once its verified purchase system is available.

During development, the public debug build uses Google test advertisements. Production advertising is currently disabled. Google Mobile Ads and the User Messaging Platform are included in the public build. When enabled and permitted, these services may process IP addresses, device and advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from an IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic information. Non-personalized advertisements do not mean no data is processed.

The app stores only an age category locally, not a birth date. No advertisements are requested for an unknown category or an under-13 selection. Ages 13-17 receive under-age-of-consent treatment; all ad requests are configured as non-personalized with a PG maximum content rating. The intended audience is ages 13 and older.

Ad requests wait for a successful Google privacy-information update and the SDK's permission to request ads. Where required, Google presents a privacy form. A Manage ad privacy choices option appears in Settings when the SDK requires it. Age category and connection retry controls are also in Settings.

6. Firebase accounts and paid Pro - planned

The public build is configured for Firebase Authentication, Firestore, and Cloud Functions. A Google sign-in and sign-out flow has been added to the local development build, but has not been distributed through Google Play. Signing in creates or accesses a Firebase Authentication account; signing out does not delete that account or local collection data. The initial Firestore rules deny client access. Google Analytics is not included in this integration.

The account system uses a Firebase user ID and Google account information, such as an email address and display name, to identify the account. Play builds also integrate Firebase App Check with Play Integrity to attest app requests; this is not a collection backup or cloud-sync feature. Local development builds also cache verified Pro access in encrypted, device-only storage, bound to the signed-in account. This cache is not included in Android backups and is cleared on sign-out. Offline access lasts up to 30 days after verification; restarting the phone requires an online check. The deployed purchase backend verifies Google Play purchase tokens and stores account-linked purchase and Pro entitlement records for restoration and fraud prevention. We do not receive your full payment-card details; Google Play processes payment.

The staged backend binds purchases to a hashed identifier derived from the verified Google account, not an email address or display name. This allows a valid permanent purchase to be restored with the same Google account after deleting and recreating an app account. An existing live account's purchase cannot be transferred to another app account. Restoring always requires server verification with Google Play.

The account-deletion and purchase-recovery backend has been deployed. Sign-in, deletion, and account recreation have been tested through the internal Play release; paid purchase recovery has not yet been validated. External email-request handling, retention practices, and Play Data safety declarations must be verified before release.

7. Deletion and retention in the current build

You can remove collection entries, folders, and decks through the app. Removing a folder leaves its cards in the collection. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local app data; exported files and Android/cloud backups may remain separately. Manage those copies through their file or backup provider.

Technical data handled by Google and other providers is subject to their retention practices. The account/purchase backend has been deployed for release testing; the account-enabled app is available to internal testers only. Production checkout remains disabled, and the separate checkout-test build still requires license-test validation. For privacy questions, contact sbatyapps@gmail.com. Do not send passwords, payment details, or service-account keys.

In the staged account-enabled app, Delete app account on the Pro page requires a recent confirmation with the same Google account. It removes the Firebase account, signs out, and clears cached Pro access; it preserves local cards, folders, and decks. It does not delete your Google account or automatically refund purchases. You can also request account deletion through support without reinstalling the app; account ownership must be verified first.

For recovery and fraud prevention, a minimal record containing a hashed Google-account identifier, purchase token, and verification status is retained while a permanent Pro purchase remains valid. This retained record has no email address, display name, or collection data. Revoked records are removed during deletion; later cancellations are removed after server verification, subject to service availability. Pending cleanup retains a temporary Firebase account ID; completion replaces it with a hash-only marker and completion time, removed by hourly cleanup after 24 hours. Service interruptions may delay cleanup. Support correspondence is deleted from the developer's mailbox after resolving the request; provider backups, service logs, and Google Play transaction records follow the respective provider's retention policies.
