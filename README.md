# MTG Scan & Build

An Android app that scans your Magic: The Gathering cards with the phone camera, keeps a full inventory of your collection, and builds playable decks for every legal format using only cards you own.

## Features

- **Mana themes and land wallpapers:** Appearance settings offer White/Plains, Blue/Island, Black/Swamp, Red/Mountain, Green/Forest, and Colorless/Wastes. Each uses an original illustrated landscape at 18% opacity behind every page except Scan, including Home's News, Banned/Restricted, and Rulebook tabs and card/deck details. Cards, dialogs, toolbars, and navigation retain solid surfaces for readability. Artwork is bundled as local drawing code, works offline, and uses no card art. Dark, Light, and Follow phone remain separate **Display mode** options; the optional Android 12+ phone-color theme uses Wastes artwork. Existing saved accent selections are preserved; the former Arcane purple option is now Colorless/Wastes.

- **Fully unlocked:** Pro purchases are paused. All features, including unlimited folders, generated decks, Moxfield comparison, CSV import/export, and the opening-page picker, are available without a purchase. Small banner ads appear outside the Scan screen, subject to age, consent, and ad availability.
- **Home (opening page):** tabs for current Wizards of the Coast news headlines, banned/restricted cards, and a rulebook. News opens the original official articles. Banned and restricted lists load live from Scryfall, grouped by tabletop/1v1, multiplayer, and Arena formats, with card search, separate restricted lists, refresh controls, and last-fetched times. These are current legality snapshots, not ban history; cards excluded by rotation or format eligibility are not labeled banned. Official Wizards lists and announcements are linked for effective dates and tournament decisions. Rulebook includes original quick references for Constructed, Commander, Oathbreaker, Arena/Brawl, Draft, Sealed, and Two-Headed Giant, plus links to current official basic, comprehensive, format, and tournament rules. Full documents/articles open in the browser; quick references work offline. Home feeds require internet; refresh failures are shown explicitly, and an already-loaded snapshot remains visible with a warning.
- **Camera scanning:** on-device text recognition (ML Kit) reads the card name inside an on-screen frame and fuzzy-matches it against every card name on Scryfall. Includes tap-to-focus, pinch-to-zoom, a flashlight toggle, optional auto-add, a scan sound with vibration, and Undo.
- **Scan sounds:** choose from Chime, Classic scanner beep, Coin, Mana sparkle, Bell ding, Laser, Deep thump, or your own audio file. Sounds use the media volume, so they work on vibrate.
- **Photo and search:** recognize a card from a gallery picture, or search by name.
- **Collection:** quantities, foils, set/printing choice, filters by color/type/set, search by name, type or rules text, CSV/list import and CSV export. You can sort by set (A–Z or newest first) with set headers, or by value.
- **User Created Sets:** inside Collection, switch between the full inventory and your own folders. Rounded folder cards use your chosen color along their edge, with a summary ring showing value by folder and an **All collection** shortcut. Create, rename, delete, and color folders with a color wheel (plus hue, saturation, and brightness sliders). Each folder shows its card count and estimated USD value, including quantities and foil pricing; cards without prices are flagged and excluded from the estimate. Open a folder and use **Add cards** to move existing entries, or move a card from its details. Each printing/finish entry, including all its copies, belongs to at most one folder. Deleting a folder keeps its cards in **Unfiled**. Scans and imports of new entries start unfiled; additional copies keep their entry's folder. Changing printing/finish into an already-owned entry merges copies into that entry's folder. Folders and colors are saved on-device; CSV import/export remains inventory-only and does not include folder assignments.
- **TCGplayer prices:** TCGplayer market prices (normal and foil, via Scryfall) for each card, the whole collection and every deck, plus a link to the card on TCGplayer. Prices refresh daily (this can be turned off in Settings) or on demand.
- **Deck builder:** generates decks from your collection for Standard, Pioneer, Modern, Legacy, Vintage, Pauper, Commander, Duel Commander, PDH, PreDH, Oathbreaker, Brawl, Standard Brawl, and the Arena formats. Every deck is checked for format legality and ownership, and shows its mana curve. Each card shows which sets your copies come from, and you can tap a card to see its picture. Pick colors with mana-symbol buttons (W, BL, BK, R, G, or CL for a colorless deck).
- **Moxfield comparison:** search popular Moxfield decks by format (or paste a deck link). Each deck shows what percentage of it you already own, which cards are missing, and what the missing cards cost. You can save a deck to your list or copy the missing cards for TCGplayer Mass Entry.
- **Settings tab:** scan sound, volume, auto-add, vibration, keep-screen-on, start with the light on, and recognition strictness. Also display mode (dark/light/follow phone), mana theme and matching land wallpaper (including optional Android 12+ phone colors), card pictures on or off, default deck format and basic-lands default. You can also update the card-name list and clear the image cache.

## Install

Download the APK from the [Releases](../../releases) page, copy it to your phone, allow "Install unknown apps", and open it. The first launch needs internet to download the card-name list (one time). Requires Android 8.0 (API 26) or newer.

GitHub updates are installed manually. The public APK uses `com.mtgscanbuild` and the normal app icon, not the developer edition. Google Play may sign its version with a different key, preventing an in-place switch between Play and GitHub installations. Before uninstalling, export your collection to CSV. CSV does not back up folders, saved decks, or settings; uninstalling deletes local app data.

Privacy policy: https://djgizmo3-maker.github.io/mtg-scan-build/privacy/

## Build variants and Google Play preparation

Requirements: JDK 17 and the Android SDK (API 36).

```
gradlew.bat :app:assembleDeveloperRelease  # Fully unlocked personal testing APK
gradlew.bat :app:assemblePlayDebug         # Unlocked testing APK with sample ads
gradlew.bat :app:assemblePlayRelease       # Public GitHub APK; private signing still required
gradlew.bat :app:bundlePlayRelease         # Public app bundle; release signing still required
```

On macOS/Linux use `./gradlew` instead of `gradlew.bat`.

The unlocked APK is `app/build/outputs/apk/developer/release/app-developer-release.apk`. It is labeled **MTG Scan Build DEV**, uses application ID `com.mtgscanbuild.developer`, and is debug-key signed for sideloading. Its database and preferences are separate from the public app, so it does not replace or automatically copy the existing collection. The Basic testing APK is `app/build/outputs/apk/play/debug/app-play-debug.apk` and retains `com.mtgscanbuild`. Back up inventory using the previous unlocked app's CSV export before replacing it with the Basic build if needed.

Developer unlock is a build-flavor constant, not a `DEBUG` flag, hidden menu, preference, account email, or purchase callback. Even **Play debug** is Basic. Developer bundle tasks deliberately fail; never upload the developer APK to Google Play. Public release builds no longer fall back to the debug signing key. Configure a real upload key through secure local/CI signing configuration and enroll in Play App Signing; do not commit signing secrets. Old unflavored APKs in build output or at the repository root are not these new variants.

- `proPurchaseAccount` returns a domain-separated SHA-256 of the Google subject from verified Firebase authentication claims, rejects non-Google sessions, and refuses accounts with a deletion fence. Future Android checkout must pass that exact value to Billing's `setObfuscatedAccountId` before purchase. Legacy/unbound tokens are rejected rather than automatically assigning them to whichever account submits them. A detached purchase can be restored by a recreated Firebase account only with the same verified Google identity and a valid Play purchase; an existing live owner's purchase cannot transfer.
- `deleteProAccount` requires enforced App Check and Google authentication no older than five minutes. A transaction fence blocks old purchase claims/updates, then the backend deletes Firebase Authentication and removes revoked purchases or detaches minimal recovery records. Detachment removes the Firebase UID and entitlement dates; it retains the hashed Google identity, token, and verification status while the purchase is valid. Scheduled reconciliation and RTDN remove detached records after confirmed cancellation without granting access. Partial deletions retain a pending marker and retry hourly; one failed deletion does not stop other deletions or purchase checks. Completed hash-only markers are removed after 24 hours by hourly cleanup, subject to service availability.
- `verifyProPurchase` accepts a purchase token only; package and product are fixed server-side. Google Play must report a purchased, single-quantity, unconsumed Pro product bound to the caller. The backend claims the token transactionally, acknowledges the permanent upgrade, and confirms acknowledgement before granting access. Pending/canceled purchases never grant access.
- `refreshProEntitlement` rechecks the account's registered purchases with Play. Successful checks issue a server timestamp and exact 30-day expiry. Partial failures are logged and explicitly returned as `verificationIncomplete`, preserving confirmed refunds and existing server expiry rather than hiding revocations behind an unrelated failed API call.
- Android validates the server response and translates its remaining lifetime into an Android elapsed-realtime deadline anchored before the network request. The lease is observable across all existing UI/repository gates and ads; clearing it immediately removes Pro access without deleting saved data. Refresh occurs on sign-in and activity resume (automatic attempts throttled to once per minute), with a manual refresh on the Pro page. Account-generation checks discard late responses after account changes/sign-out. Refunds affect the device when its next backend response confirms them; background RTDN is not a direct push to the Android app.
- Android caches only the account UID, boot counter, elapsed-time anchor and deadline in an AES-GCM authenticated file under `noBackupFilesDir`, encrypted by an Android Keystore key. It never caches a plain preference granting Pro. Up to 30 days offline is supported across app-process restarts within the same phone boot; the user approved requiring an online check after a phone restart. Account mismatch, expiry, backwards monotonic time, tampering, or sign-out cannot restore a cached lease. Network failures do not extend access. Changing the phone's calendar date cannot extend the monotonic deadline. Cache errors are logged by class and surfaced in the Pro UI.
- `reconcileProPurchases` rechecks stored purchases hourly and recovers interrupted acknowledgements. `playPurchaseNotification` rechecks known purchases on Pub/Sub notifications, including cancellations; notification payloads never grant access directly. Per-token expiring transaction leases prevent concurrent verification/notification workers from overwriting each other's decisions.
- Purchase tokens are stored only in server-accessible Firestore records for restoration/reconciliation and are not written to logs. `firestore.rules` denies all client reads/writes; callable functions require Firebase Authentication and App Check. App Check attestation still needs validation through Play internal testing before these endpoints can be used. There is no collection/deck cloud sync.

## Tech

Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, CameraX, ML Kit Text Recognition, Room, OkHttp, Coil. Card data, images and TCGplayer market prices come from the [Scryfall API](https://scryfall.com/docs/api).
